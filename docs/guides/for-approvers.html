For approvers

How to review the actions the agent has held for a human decision.

Guides: Overview For support reps For approvers

Sign in

Open the console and choose Sign in as manager. Only a manager can approve or deny.

Review the queue

The Approvals tab lists everything waiting. Each item shows:

  • What the agent wants to do (e.g. refunds.issue) and the exact details (order and amount).
  • Why it needs approval (e.g. “refund of 200 is above the auto-approval limit of 50”).
  • Who it's for — the rep whose authority the agent is using.

Approve to let it run, or deny to stop it. The agent's run continues or ends immediately, and the decision is recorded.

You cannot approve your own request

If the agent acted on your behalf, someone else must approve it. This separation of duties is enforced, not just a guideline.

What you're actually approving

An approval is tied to that exact request — the tool, the order, and the amount. Approving a $200 refund cannot be replayed to issue $2,000, or to a different order. If the details change, it needs a fresh approval.

Reading the audit trail

The Audit tab shows one line per event, newest first. Each names the agent, the person, the tool, and the decision. For example:

tool.allowed    agent=agent  user=alice    tool=refunds.issue     require_approval
approval.decided agent=-     user=manager  tool=refunds.issue     approved
tool.allowed    agent=agent  user=alice    tool=refunds.issue     allow

Together these answer, in order: the agent asked, the manager approved, and the refund was allowed. Tokens and personal data never appear here — they are redacted before anything is written.

Why this is safe to rely on

The approver is a real, authenticated identity, and the decision is bound to the exact action. There is no way to “approve in general”, and no way to approve something other than what was reviewed.

All data in this demo is synthetic.
